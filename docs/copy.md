### FROM EXECUTIVE EXPERIENCE TO LEADERSHIP IMPACT

Jeannette Mills has spent her career leading within complex organizations where decisions, relationships, and results MATTER (or bold).
Her leadership journey ultimately included serving in senior executive roles at Tennessee Valley Authority, beginning her tenure as the Executive Vice President and Chief External Relations Officer ultimately becoming the Executive Vice President and Chief Administrative Officer. Her experience extends beyond the executive suite through corporate and nonprofit board service, mentoring, speaking, and the development of emerging and established leaders.
Her work is grounded in a simple principle: leadership is not only about what you accomplish; it is also about your enduring impact on people throughout the journey.
Today, Jeannette brings those lessons directly to organizations, leadership teams, and professionals who understand that stronger leadership creates stronger organizations.

### LEADERSHIP DEVELOPMENT BUILT FOR THE REAL WORLD

Leadership becomes most important when circumstances become difficult.
Organizations need leaders who can communicate clearly, make sound decisions, navigate change, build trust, manage competing priorities, develop talent, and maintain performance without losing sight of the people responsible for producing the results.
Jeannette’s training and coaching draw from decades of firsthand leadership experience rather than theory alone. Her programs help leaders examine how they show up, how they influence others, and how their everyday leadership behaviors affect organizational culture and performance.

### FROM TOMME’S GIRL TO LEADING BOLDLY

Long before Jeannette Mills became a senior executive, she was Tomme’s girl.
Her story began with humble roots and grew into a career marked by achievement, difficult decisions, persistence, leadership, and lessons that could never have been learned from a textbook alone.
Tomme’s Girl tells the story behind the leader.
LEAD BOLDLY™ takes the leadership lessons within that story and turns them into practical principles leaders can examine and apply within their own lives, teams, and organizations.
Together, they create more than a memoir. They create a leadership development experience.
